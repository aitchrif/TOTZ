How FORGE works

The public workflow is simple. FORGE does not need custody of your NFT collection or reward wallet.

Mainnet release policy

Public Beta does not mean unrestricted contract deployment.

SAFETY
New Robinhood Mainnet deploy / fund / publish actions remain operator-controlled during Public Beta. Public users can use X-RAY and EPOCHS, prepare their distribution and Merkle package, and review the resulting data. A Mainnet claim release is only opened through a controlled release window with server-side verification.
